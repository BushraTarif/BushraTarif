## About Me

I am a lecturer at **Beaconhouse International College** and an MS graduate. I teach Artificial Intelligence, Databases and Data Science, and I build and train AI models in my own research.

I believe the best way to understand a model is to build it, break it, and then explain it to a classroom. This GitHub is where I share that work openly: code, notebooks, experiments and results.

## Research Interests

- **Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)** and how people experience technology
- **Deep learning** for real-world problems
- **Remote sensing**: satellite and radar (SAR) imagery for climate and disaster response

## Current Research

> **Multimodal Deep Learning for Probabilistic Flood Forecasting Using Hydrological, Meteorological, and Satellite Data**
>
> Combining river data, weather data and satellite images in one deep learning model to predict floods, with an estimate of how certain each prediction is.
